# Invest IQ Multi-Factor Scoring Methodology

## 1. Core Principles
- **Explainable & Rule-Based**: The multi-factor scoring model is fully deterministic and rule-based, not an uninterpretable machine learning model.
- **Decision-Support Indicator**: The generated 0–100 score and categorical labels (Buy, Hold, Sell) are analytical indicators for comparing assets, **not** personalized financial advice or return guarantees.
- **Zero $\neq$ Null**: Missing financial metrics are never imputed as zero; instead, absent factors are dynamically excluded and existing weights renormalized.

---

## 2. Factor Architecture & Base Weights

The scoring system aggregates 4 distinct pillars:

| Pillar | Base Weight | Target Range / Signal | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Technical Trend** | 30% | Price vs SMA 20, Price vs SMA 50, SMA 20 vs 50 | Measures structural price trend alignment and bullish/bearish crossover state. |
| **Momentum** | 20% | RSI (14-period Wilder) | Measures overbought/oversold and intermediate momentum (optimal zone 45–65). |
| **Fundamentals** | 30% | P/E, P/B, Operating Margin, ROE, Debt/Equity | Balances valuation multiples with profitability and balance-sheet quality. |
| **Risk & Volatility** | 20% | Annualized Volatility ($\sigma \times \sqrt{252}$), Max Drawdown | Rewards lower volatility and controlled historical drawdown. |

---

## 3. Dynamic Renormalization Formula

When one or more factors are unavailable (for example, if a company has negative earnings yielding no P/E, or missing balance sheet ratios), the model does not penalize the stock by assigning zero. Instead, it reweights the active factors:

$$W_i^* = \frac{W_i}{\sum_{k \in \mathcal{A}} W_k}$$

Where $\mathcal{A}$ is the set of available factors, and $W_i$ is the initial base weight of factor $i$.

The composite score is then calculated as:

$$\text{Final Score} = \sum_{i \in \mathcal{A}} \left( S_i \times W_i^* \right)$$

Where $S_i \in [0, 100]$ is the normalized factor score for pillar $i$.

## 4. Pillar Scoring Formulations

### A. Technical Trend (30%)
Calculated from 3 indicators:
1. **Price > SMA 20**: +35 points if true, else 10.
2. **Price > SMA 50**: +35 points if true, else 10.
3. **SMA 20 > SMA 50 (Golden cross alignment)**: +30 points if true, else 10.
Bounded within $[10, 100]$.

### B. Momentum (20%) - RSI
Based on 14-period RSI:
- $RSI \in [45, 60]$: Bullish healthy momentum $\rightarrow$ 90–100 pts.
- $RSI \in [35, 45]$ or $[60, 70]$: Moderate momentum $\rightarrow$ 70–85 pts.
- $RSI > 70$: Overbought risk territory $\rightarrow$ 35–50 pts.
- $RSI < 30$: Oversold bounce potential vs severe downtrend $\rightarrow$ 40–55 pts.
- Extremes ($RSI > 85$ or $RSI < 20$): 20–30 pts.

### C. Fundamentals & Valuation (30%)
Synthesizes available metrics:
- **P/E ratio**: Evaluated in context (10–25 ideal for general equities, >50 considered stretched, <0 omitted from valuation bonus).
- **ROE (Return on Equity)**: >15% yields top tier score (85–100), 8–15% moderate (60–80), <0 penalizes (20–40).
- **Operating Margin**: >20% strong (90), 10–20% healthy (75), <0 unprofitable (30).
- **Debt-to-Equity**: <0.8 healthy (90), 0.8–2.0 acceptable (65), >2.5 elevated leverage (35).

### D. Risk & Volatility (20%)
1. **Annualized Volatility**:
   - $\le 18\%$: Low risk $\rightarrow$ 90 pts.
   - $18\% - 30\%$: Moderate risk $\rightarrow$ 70 pts.
   - $30\% - 45\%$: High risk $\rightarrow$ 45 pts.
   - $> 45\%$: Very high volatility $\rightarrow$ 25 pts.
2. **Historical Max Drawdown**:
   - Drawdown $> -15\%$: 90 pts.
   - Drawdown $-15\%$ to $-30\%$: 70 pts.
   - Drawdown $-30\%$ to $-50\%$: 45 pts.
   - Drawdown $< -50\%$: 20 pts.
Combined average forms the Risk score.

## 5. Classification Labels & Disclaimers

| Score Range | Experimental Label | Plain-Language Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| **$\ge 70$** | **Bullish / Buy Signal** | Favorable alignment across active technical, fundamental, and risk metrics. |
| **$50 - 69$** | **Neutral / Hold Signal** | Balanced profile with mixed technicals or average valuation/risk metrics. |
| **$< 50$** | **Caution / Sell Signal** | Deteriorating trend, high volatility, overextended valuation, or weak fundamentals. |

> **IMPORTANT DISCLAIMER**: All scores, breakdowns, and signals generated by Invest IQ are purely mathematical decision-support heuristics. They do not constitute investment advice, financial planning, or performance guarantees.
